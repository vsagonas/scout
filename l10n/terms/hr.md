# Scout in Croatian (hr): term list

Machine translation, no native reviewer yet. Polite plural "vi" in sentences (Odaberite, Dodajte, Pokušajte), as the Croatian App Store text does. Menu items and buttons are the imperative singular the way Apple's Croatian macOS writes them (Otvori, Zatvori, Spremi, Odustani). Words match the App Store text, except Tray (see below).

Brand names are never declined: "aplikacije Scout", "u alatu Codex", "u Keychain". "notch" and "Mac" take the usual case endings as the store text does ("u notchu", "na Macu").

Pixel labels use capitals and no diacritics (the 5x7 font has none): CIJELI DAN, SPAVA, CAO %@ (BYE), POVEZIVANJE, GOTOVO, OMILJENO, ZAMRZNUTO, SCOUT VAS POZDRAVLJA, NAJNOVIJE, POPIS, POPISI, TREBA VAS, NEMA AGENATA, NEMA SIGNALA, PAUZIRANO, MJESTA, SPREMNO, POKRECE SE, ZAUSTAVLJENO, DOBRODOSLI %@, TIJEK, RADI.

Style: „ ” quotes, "…" as one character, no dashes as separators (a colon instead: "Fokus: %@"), Western digits, "posto" for spoken percentages.

Choices made while translating:
- Left/right for AirPods: L / D (lijevo / desno). Case = Kutijica.
- Counted words avoid the Croatian plural rules ("Agenti: 3", "Dosad sesija: 3", "%lld min").
- "Ask anything" (the Ask AI field placeholder, 12 characters at most) = "Pitajte" (7).
- "Feedback" is kept in the English form for length (Apple's "Povratne informacije" is 250% of it).
