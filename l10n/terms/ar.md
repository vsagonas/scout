Quotation marks « » . Punctuation ، ؛ ؟ . Digits 0-9. No dashes as separators.

Choices made while translating:
- "this Mac" = جهاز Mac هذا; "home" (the agents' den) = البيت; "Tray" = الدرج everywhere, as in the store text.
- Short labels in the notch use the short forms: التبويب (tab), ذاكرة (Mem), كتم (Mute), تبويبات (Tabs), فك التثبيت (Unpin), المايك يعمل / لا مايك (Mic on / No mic).
- The AI family (اسأل الذكاء الاصطناعي, وكلاء الذكاء الاصطناعي, استخدام الذكاء الاصطناعي) keeps the full Arabic words everywhere, so these labels are long; check them in the notch.
- One U+200E (left-to-right mark) stands before a path or command that starts with ~ or / inside Arabic text (~/.claude/projects, ~/.codex/sessions), so the bidi algorithm keeps it in order. No other direction marks.
- The → arrow of the English "Settings → General" became › (it mirrors in right-to-left text).
