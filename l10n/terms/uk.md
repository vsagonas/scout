# Scout in Ukrainian (uk): term list

Machine translation, no native reviewer yet. Formal "ви" in sentences (Виберіть, Додайте, Спробуйте, Запитайте ШІ), as the Ukrainian App Store text does. Menu items and buttons are infinitives or nouns the way Apple's Ukrainian macOS writes them (Відкрити, Закрити, Зберегти, Скасувати). Words match the App Store text, except Tray (see below).

Brand names stay Latin and are not declined: "Scout", "Codex", "Mac" ("на цьому Mac", as in the store text), "у Codex".

Pixel-font keys (l10n/PIXEL-LABELS.txt): the English word is kept exactly (the 5x7 font has no Cyrillic), like the Russian file does.

Style: « » quotes, "…" as one character, no dashes as separators (a colon instead: "Фокус: %@"), Western digits, "відсотків" for spoken percentages.

Choices made while translating:
- Left/right for AirPods: Л / П (лівий / правий). Case = Кейс.
- Counted words avoid the Ukrainian plural rules ("Агенти: 3", "Сеансів досі: 3", "%lld хв").
- "Ask anything" (the Ask AI field placeholder, 12 characters at most) = "Запитайте" (9).
