Apple app and Settings names: macOS shows them in English on a Latvian user's Mac (there is no Latvian macOS), so where a sentence sends the user to a macOS pane or app, the exact English name is kept: Privacy & Security, Full Disk Access, Screen Recording, Automation, Paste from Other Apps, Login Items, Contacts, Calendars, Notes, Messages, Music, Shortcuts, Activity Monitor, Weather.
Scout's own tab and setting names are translated and used the same everywhere (Iestatījumi › Jautāt MI).
Finder, Spotlight, Keychain stay as they are.

Pixel-font keys (l10n/PIXEL-LABELS.txt): capitals, A-Z only, no diacritics: ALL DAY = VISU DIENU, ASLEEP = MIEGA, BYE = TIKAMIES, DONE = PABEIGTS, READY = GATAVS, NEEDS YOU = VAJAG TEVI, NO AGENTS = NAV AGENTU, FROZEN = SASALIS, HELLO FROM SCOUT = SVEICIENS NO SCOUT.
Short widget titles prefer a word without diacritics where one exists (Pulss, Radio, Spogulis, Mediji, Piezīmes); where the natural word has one the pixel font draws it without the marks, which stays readable.
Brand names stay Latin: Scout, Claude, Claude Code, Codex, OpenAI, GPT, Anthropic, Mac, macOS, AirPods, AirDrop, Spotlight, Keychain, Zoom, Meet, Teams, Webex, Spotify. Digits 0-9. No dashes as separators (a colon or comma instead).
