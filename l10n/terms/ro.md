# Scout in Romanian (ro): term list

Machine translation, no native reviewer yet. Informal "tu" (imperative singular: Alege, Adaugă, Încearcă), as the Romanian App Store text does ("Vezi", "Răspunde", "Întreabă AI"). Menu items and buttons are the imperative singular the way Apple's Romanian macOS writes them (Deschide, Închide, Salvează, Anulează). Romanian diacritics with comma below (ș, ț). Words match the App Store text, except Tray (see below).

Brand names are never declined: "aplicația Scout", "în Codex". "notch" takes the hyphenated article as the store text does ("notch-ul"); Mac is "acest Mac".

Pixel labels use capitals and no diacritics (the 5x7 font has none): TOATA ZIUA, DOARME, PA %@ (BYE), CONECTARE, GATA, INTREBARE AI, FAVORITE, INGHETAT, SALUT DE LA SCOUT, RECENTE, LISTA, LISTE, TE ASTEAPTA, NICIUN AGENT, FARA SEMNAL, PAUZA, LOCURI, PREGATIT, PORNESTE, OPRIT, BUN VENIT %@, FLUX, LUCREAZA.

Style: „ ” quotes, "…" as one character, no dashes as separators (a colon instead: "Concentrare: %@"), Western digits, "procente" for spoken percentages.

Choices made while translating:
- Left/right for AirPods: S / D (stânga / dreapta). Case = Carcasă.
- Counted words avoid the Romanian plural rules ("Agenți: 3", "Sesiuni până acum: 3", "%lld min").
- "Ask anything" (the Ask AI field placeholder, 12 characters at most) = "Întreabă" (8).
- The Prompter sample text is addressed to an audience (plural "v-ați alăturat") because it is read aloud to others.
