# Turkish (tr): term list

Machine translation, no native reviewer yet. Same words as the App Store text. Informal "sen" as the store text does (bare imperatives: Seç, Aç, Ekle), labels as short imperatives or nouns the way macOS writes them.
Suffixes after Latin names and abbreviations take an apostrophe: widget'lar, Tepsi'ye, Nook'ta, YZ'ye, hook'lar. After a number the noun stays singular (3 ajan).

Quotation marks “ ”. Digits 0-9. No dashes as separators.

Choices made while translating:
- "Ask anything" (placeholder of the dashboard field, about 12 characters) = «Bir şey sor» (11).
- "Home" (the agents' den) is «ev»; "Home" the tab stays «Ana Sayfa».
- Percent follows Turkish writing: %%%lld (%25), and spoken labels read «yüzde N».
- Short notch labels: Mik. açık / Mik. yok, Sustur, Diğer, Açılır, Çalıştı, Adlandır, Link / Linkler, Zoom. Labels still over 200 % of a very short English word are Apple's own Turkish words (Uygulamalar, Sabitle, Kırmızı, Gönderildi, Zamanlayıcı, Sabitlemeyi kaldır, Ana Sayfa).
- Capital Turkish letters in the pixel font: Ç Ğ İ Ö Ş Ü are written C G I O S U (TUM GUN, BAGLANIYOR, FAVORILER, SINYAL YOK, BASLIYOR, IS AKISI, CALISIYOR); the other words need no change.
