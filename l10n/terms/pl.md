Pixel labels use capitals and no Polish letters (the 5x7 font has none): the plain Latin spelling, e.g. CALY DZIEN, SPI, PA %@ (for BYE), LACZENIE, BRAK SYGNALU.
"Ask anything" (dashboard field placeholder, at most 12 characters): "Pytaj o coś".

Where a Polish number agreement would break ("%lld open"), the text is a label with a colon: "Otwarte: %lld". "%@ finished" becomes the impersonal "%@: zakończono" because the gender of %@ is unknown.
