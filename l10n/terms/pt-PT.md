Machine translation, no native reviewer yet. European Portuguese (ecrã, ficheiro, transferir, separador, guião, "A" + infinitive for what is happening: "A transferir…"). Formal address in sentences ("Veja", "Escolha", "Pergunte", "precisa de si"), as the Portuguese App Store text does. Menu items and buttons are infinitives (Abrir, Fechar, Guardar, Cancelar) the way Apple's pt-PT macOS writes them. Words match the App Store text, except Tray (see below).

Brand names are never declined or merged with prepositions beyond the article: "o Scout", "no Codex", "a app Scout".

Pixel labels use capitals and no accents (the 5x7 font has none): TODO O DIA, A DORMIR, ADEUS %@ (BYE), A LIGAR, FEITO, FAVORITOS, CONGELADO, OLA DO SCOUT, RECENTES, LISTA, LISTAS, PRECISA DE SI, SEM AGENTES, SEM SINAL, EM PAUSA, LOCAIS, PRONTO, A INICIAR, PARADO, BEM-VINDO %@, FLUXO, A TRABALHAR.

Style: “ ” quotes, "…" as one character, no dashes as separators (a colon instead: "Foco: %@"), Western digits, "por cento" for spoken percentages.

Choices made while translating:
- Left/right for AirPods: E / D (esquerdo / direito). Case = Estojo.
- "Ask anything" (the Ask AI field placeholder, 12 characters at most) = "Pergunte" (8).
- Counted words avoid plural trouble ("Agentes: 3", "Sessões até agora: 3", "%lld min"); "%lld wpm" = "%lld ppm".
